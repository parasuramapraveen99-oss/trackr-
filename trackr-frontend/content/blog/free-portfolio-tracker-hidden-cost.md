# What a free portfolio tracker still asks you to maintain

A free tool can be useful. A paid tool can still leave gaps. The meaningful comparison is the work required to maintain records you can trust.

## Count the work around the application

Do you need to clean every broker export, repair duplicates, enter corporate actions or rebuild old acquisition history? Check those tasks with your own files rather than assuming that a price or marketing label predicts the answer.

## Understand the access you grant

Read which records are uploaded, which external services receive them and what export or deletion controls exist. A file-upload workflow does not need your broker's trading password. A protected PDF may need its document password to be opened; that is a different permission.

Do not assume that every free application sells data, or that every paid one avoids unnecessary sharing. Read the actual policy and product behaviour.

## Check what the result includes

A holdings view can be correct while the acquisition history is incomplete. An expected dividend can be useful while still not belonging in realised income. A fund-statement preview can read a document without importing transactions.

# Looking for an MProfit alternative? Start with the reports you need

Before changing software, write down the reports you actually use. A replacement for a holdings dashboard may not replace a family office's accounting and reporting process.

[MProfit's official site](https://mprofit.in/) describes consolidated investment reporting, transaction processing and capital-gains reports. Those are meaningful capabilities to compare against your requirements, rather than assumptions about a competitor's missing features.

## What to test in Trackr+

Use a supported tradebook or contract note to check three things:

- Holdings and acquisition history agree with the records you imported.
- Your return comparison uses the same period and cash flows on both sides.
- Missing prices or acquisitions are named, including when the rest of the portfolio can still be analysed.

Trackr+ derives its financial figures from the transaction ledger. Reconciliation compares that ledger with supplied evidence; matching quantities alone does not certify costs or the complete trading history.

## Be explicit about what must carry over

Check every asset class, report format, family/client permission and accounting export you depend on. A sample family-portfolio screen is not a working replacement for those capabilities. The mutual-fund statement checker in Trackr+ currently provides a preview rather than a persistent transaction import.

Obtain current prices and plan restrictions from the providers. Compare the cost of importing and checking your records as well as the subscription.

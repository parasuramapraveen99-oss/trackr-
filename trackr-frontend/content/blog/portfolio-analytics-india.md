# Portfolio analytics: the question behind each number

A useful analytics page explains a question, its inputs and its limits. Several different numbers can all be correct while describing different things.

## How did my money perform?

XIRR uses the dates and amounts of your cash flows. A time-weighted return links portfolio returns while neutralising those flows. Neither is simply the percentage change in your current holdings' value.

Trackr+ calculates these figures in its financial services; an explanation generated from them does not create a missing price or transaction.

## Where is my exposure concentrated?

Holding weights and sector allocations describe the portfolio at a stated date. A large holding can influence the result even when the number of securities looks comfortably high.

Company-quality and valuation measures need dated company inputs. A missing ratio remains unavailable. A health grade is a descriptive, experimental rubric rather than an investment recommendation or assessment of your personal suitability.

## What did past risk look like?

Read the stated period and basis. Risk based on recorded portfolio history and a simulation using today's weights answer different questions. Volatility, drawdown and a historical loss estimate are descriptions of observed data, not guarantees about the next month.

## What was left out?

Trackr+ can calculate on a usable subset when a holding lacks a price or complete acquisition history. Read the named exclusions and coverage beside that result. A partial figure should not look like a complete portfolio total.

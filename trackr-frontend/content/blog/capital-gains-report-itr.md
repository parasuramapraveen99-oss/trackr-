# Preparing a capital-gains report: keep the evidence behind each sale

A capital-gains report connects a disposal with the acquisition history that supports its cost and holding period. A holdings screen alone cannot do that: investments sold during the year may no longer appear there.

The Income Tax Department describes [Schedule CG](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/schedule_cg) as the part of the return used to report capital gains. Use the official form and instructions for the relevant assessment year when preparing a return.

## Start from the underlying records

Keep the purchase and sale dates, quantities, consideration, charges and supporting documents. Where applicable, corporate-action terms and historical valuation evidence can also affect the calculation. Do not replace an unknown acquisition price with a market close simply to make the report complete.

## What Trackr+ contributes

Trackr+'s equity calculations match sales to acquisition lots and use recorded corporate actions and the supported tax rules. Reports name missing inputs rather than treating them as zero. A statement-quantity match is useful evidence, but it does not by itself verify acquisition costs or tax treatment.

Check the report's stated scope. A mutual-fund statement preview does not create fund transactions, and an equity report should not be assumed to cover every other asset or derivative ledger.

## Before using a report

Review excluded sales, unmatched history, financial-year boundaries and the source of every cost adjustment. Compare the output with broker records and the relevant official instructions. Ask your tax professional to resolve classification or filing questions; this article does not prescribe tax rates or determine your liability.
